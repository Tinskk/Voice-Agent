# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you take reservations?

Yes — call in and the assistant can check availability and book a table for
you on the spot.

## Can I order for pickup instead of delivery?

Yes, just let us know when you're ordering.

## Do you cater private events?

Yes — tell us about your event (date, approximate size) and someone will
follow up with a quote.

## Can I pay over the phone?

Not yet — payment is collected at pickup or on delivery.

## Can I change or cancel an order after placing it?

Call back as soon as possible; whether it can still be changed depends on how
far along it is.
